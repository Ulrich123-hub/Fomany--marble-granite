About Fomany

Reliable stone solutions for residential and commercial projects.

Fomany Marble & Granite Company Ltd is based on Port Harcourt Road, across Apams, Owerri, Imo State, Nigeria. We supply different kinds of marble, granite and other finishing stones sourced through suppliers in Lagos and other parts of Nigeria.


Whether you are building a home, hotel, office, shop or outdoor space, our catalogue gives you options for countertops, floors, walls, facades, bathrooms, compounds and decorative projects.
